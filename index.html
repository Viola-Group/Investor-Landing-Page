Viola Ventures is an early-stage fund committed to building global category leaders from Israel. Founded in 2000, Viola Ventures has backed 200+ technology companies, among them some of Israel's leading startups such as ironSource (acquired by Unity), Redis, Lightricks, Immunai, and Pagaya.


With over $1.5B AUM, the fund invests from the inception stage, with a focus on Cyber, FinTech, Vertical AI, Infrastructure, and defense.


Viola Ventures is part of Viola Group, Israel's leading tech investment platform, managing over $7B AUM.
