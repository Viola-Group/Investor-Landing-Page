We're excited to announce Viola's NYC Cyber Tour, our upcoming U.S. roadshow taking place May 26–28, 2026.

We're convening a select group of CISOs and security leaders in New York for a series of focused, off-the-record discussions on the evolving cybersecurity landscape.

The program is designed to facilitate peer exchange around real-world challenges, priorities, and emerging threats, alongside direct engagement with a curated set of founders building across key cyber domains.

This is a unique opportunity to connect with Israeli founders shaping the next wave of cybersecurity innovation and explore solutions at the forefront of the market, in a setting built for candid dialogue and meaningful relationships.

Aryon Security

Cloud security today is largely reactive, leaving organizations to deal with issues only after they reach production. This reality quietly but consistently impacts organizations, creating operational drag, increasing organizational risk, and forcing security and engineering teams into resource-intensive remediation cycles. The result is high MTTRs, growing backlogs of unresolved issues, and ongoing friction between teams.

Aryon defines the standard for enterprise-grade cloud security enforcement. Their platform enables gradual, granular, and production-safe enforcement, setting a new baseline for how organizations prevent cloud security gaps. By enforcing policies at deployment — whether through IaC or ClickOps — Aryon stops issues before they reach production, reducing organizational risk and freeing teams from resource-intensive remediation cycles.

With over $1.5B AUM, the fund invests from the inception stage, with a focus on Cyber, FinTech, Vertical AI, Infrastructure, and defense.
